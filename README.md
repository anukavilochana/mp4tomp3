# MP4 to MP3 Converter

A fast, completely free, and 100% private web-based tool to convert your video files into high-quality audio directly in your browser. Because processing happens entirely on your device, your files are never uploaded to a server.

**[Live Demo](https://anukavilochana.github.io/mp4tomp3/)**

## Features

* **100% Private & Secure:** Files are processed locally in your browser using client-side technology. Your data never leaves your device.
* **Lightning Fast:** Instant conversions with zero server round-trip delays or queue wait times.
* **Batch Conversion:** Drag and drop multiple files at once to convert them simultaneously.
* **Custom Audio Bitrates:** Choose the perfect balance between quality and file size:
  * 128 kbps (Standard)
  * 192 kbps (Good quality - Recommended)
  * 320 kbps (Best quality)
* **Zero Limits:** No registrations, no subscriptions, no daily limits, and no watermarks.
* **Multiple Formats Supported:** Works seamlessly with MP4, MOV, WebM, and M4V video formats.
* **Bulk Download:** Save files individually or download all converted tracks at once in a convenient ZIP archive.

## How It Works

1. **Upload:** Drag and drop your video files into the drop zone, or click to browse your device.
2. **Configure:** Select your desired audio bitrate quality (128kbps, 192kbps, or 320kbps).
3. **Convert:** Click **Convert All Files** to process them instantly.
4. **Download:** Preview your audio tracks and click download individually, or click **Download All** to grab the ZIP file.

## Technologies Used
